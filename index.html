Task 1D: Film Reviews – The Red Balloon (1956)

Read the four reviews (A–D) of Albert Lamorisse’s classic film The Red Balloon and match each question (1–4) with the correct reviewer.

Reviewer A

The timeless tale of Albert Lamorisse’s short film tells the story of the balloon as it follows a lonely schoolboy around Paris. The visual power of the balloon is essential, becoming the true star of the movie. Filmed in simple language, the final scene, as wondrous a finale as ever has been put to film, suggests promise. This is a film for grown-ups too, and perhaps nostalgia allows us to overlook the cliched dialogue and the wires keeping the balloon afloat.

Reviewer B

I was enchanted by it as a kid, and on reflection, it really is a film just for that age group. My adult sensibilities left me wanting more as the film drew to a close. This is no fault of the film but more in the casting of the director's son in the starring role: he is clearly a novice and is never convincing. Perhaps the most innovative element is telling a story with practically no dialogue, using music instead of a narrator.

Reviewer C

The key to its lasting endurance is its simplicity. Lamorisse captures events through an innocent's eyes, with his son in the leading role, using visual imagery that appeals to younger viewers without ever talking down to the audience, which is why it remains appreciated by fans as they grow old. As the story reaches its unexpected yet satisfying conclusion, the director’s lack of embellishment and his son’s superb delivery create an enduring fable.

Reviewer D

There’s power in how it tells the classic tale of the human spirit triumphing over hardship. Making his debut at six, Pascal Lamorisse, the exceptional young actor, manages to deliver the very few scripted lines with true emotion. It’s difficult to imagine anyone else in the role. The film has delighted viewers from youngsters to grandparents. For the more mature viewer, there’s a slight sense of inevitability regarding the climax, which somewhat takes the shine off that moment.
